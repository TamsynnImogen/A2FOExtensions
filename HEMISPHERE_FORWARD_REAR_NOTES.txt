A2FOExtensions directionalShields mode 4
========================================

Mode 4 adds two 180-degree shield hemispheres:
  forward / aft

SPR entry expected by A2FOCraftIdentity:
  dsh shield_hemi 0 0 128 128

The same upper-half mask is used for both facings; the aft copy is flipped
vertically at draw time. Port/starboard are inactive (0/0). Existing
Forward/Aft Area and value-text GUI CFG overrides remain available.
