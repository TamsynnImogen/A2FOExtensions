capture remain in the roster. The Borg choice comes from fresh logical race

model/authored/node/material data owners are accepted; replacement owners are
rejected. Callback and allocation failures preserve the caller's output.

frame. Access requires the owner thread, current named-operation signatures,
current parent source/lifecycle tickets and exact baseline/class/texture owners.

preserving independently valid parent data. Disable/rearm clears it. A discarded
snapshot needs a fresh frame; restoring a pointer does not republish it.
