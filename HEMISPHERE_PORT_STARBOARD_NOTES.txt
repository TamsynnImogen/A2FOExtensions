A2FOExtensions directionalShields mode 5
========================================

Mode 5 adds two 180-degree shield hemispheres:
  port / starboard

SPR entry expected by A2FOCraftIdentity:
  dshs shield_hemi_side 0 0 128 128

`shield_hemi_side.tga` should be a full 128x128 left/port hemisphere mask.
The starboard copy is mirrored horizontally at draw time. Forward/aft are
inactive (0/0). Existing Port/Starboard Area and value-text GUI CFG overrides
remain available.
